Privacy policy

Obsidian finance is a private tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

Data accessed

With the read-only Gmail permission (gmail.readonly) the tool searches the owner's mailbox for purchase receipts and reads those messages. It never sends, deletes or modifies email.

How the data is used

Receipt details - date, seller, purchased items and amounts - are written to the owner's personal Google spreadsheet to analyse household spending. No other use is made of the data.

Storage and sharing

Data is kept only in the owner's Google account and on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and is not used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on the app's Google consent screen.
